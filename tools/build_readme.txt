Silicon City
============

A city builder on the real land around Menlo Park and the San Francisco Bay.
Press H in the game for the guide.

Opening it the first time
-------------------------
The game isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft, so your computer will warn you
the first time. This is normal for small free games.

Mac: double-click "Silicon City". If it says it can't be opened, open
System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway",
then open the game again.

Windows: double-click "Silicon City.exe". If Windows says it protected your
PC, click "More info", then "Run anyway".

It needs a fairly recent computer: big cities are heavy.

Your saved city is kept with the game's settings on your computer, not next
to the game, so you can replace the game with a newer version safely.
